Curatorial Work

Exhibitions Melanie has curated, produced, and organized — from a special-collections show at RISD's Fleet Library to an international touring exhibition in China.

RISD Fleet Library Special Collections Exhibition

Exhibition Curator

Co-curated the exhibition “This is a book?!”, leading concept development, object selection, and label writing in collaboration with a student team. Coordinated with Fleet Library and RISD Museum curatorial professionals on research and exhibition strategies. Managed project timelines and team deliverables to ensure the exhibition was prepared for its opening.

RISD GD Commons Exhibition

Exhibition Publisher

Led the visual identity, way-finding system, and catalog development for the exhibition “From Top To...”, from concept through final production and distribution. Coordinated with RISD GD Typeshop and design teams to execute publications and exhibition materials, conducted artist interviews, and compiled editorial content for the catalog and promotional use.

“回像” (Hui Xiang) China Touring Exhibition — Hangzhou

Project Lead — co-organized by RISD CSSA and China Academy of Art CSSA

Managed end-to-end project execution across five departments — Artist Relations, Media & Editorial, Equipment Coordination, On-Site Exhibition Operations, and Exhibition Design — to ensure every milestone was delivered on schedule. Planned and coordinated the opening ceremony, including programming, logistics, guest invitations, and catering, and secured coverage from 20+ media outlets, including Zhejiang Daily, Hangzhou Daily, and Artron.net.

“玩儿闹” (Wan'er Nao) Four-School Joint Exhibition — Beijing

Exhibition Operations & Artist Relations — co-organized by RISD CSSA, UAL CSSA, CAFA, and Tsinghua University

Served as the primary contact for 15+ artists, coordinating fine art logistics, contract completion, and documentation, and conducted media outreach that led to coverage in People's Daily and NetEase News. Oversaw exhibition operations by recruiting and managing volunteers, monitoring the liaison team's progress, and supporting curatorial planning and installation.

Sotheby's Institute of Art Exhibition

Artist Liaison

Supported pre-conference planning and guest management for “Ordinary Magic: Beyond Everyday”, coordinating logistics and bilingual communication for an immersive exhibition featuring 18 international artists.
