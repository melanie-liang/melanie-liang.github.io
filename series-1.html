Blessing (祝福)祝福（Blessing）

2026 · Digital illustration2026 · 数字插画

This short picture-book project is based on my own experience — living alone abroad in my twenties, often anxious about the future, but held together by the words my mother gave me. She told me about a dream she had during pregnancy — a dream that suggested I was someone special, someone who would shine. She also told me that my grandfather would bless me from above. These two things have stayed with me, and they've always made me feel safe. I used ginkgo leaves as a recurring motif for healing and family harmony, placed in circles to suggest wholeness and protection. The warm yellow palette and soft watercolor-like textures create a gentle, dreamlike atmosphere — a visual language that reflects the comfort of inherited love.这本短篇绘本源自我自己的经历——二十多岁独自在国外生活，常常为未来感到不安，却一直被妈妈对我说过的话支撑着。她告诉我，怀我的时候她做过一个梦，梦里预示我会是一个特别的人，一个会发光的人。她还告诉我，姥爷会在天上保佑我。这两件事一直留在我心里，总让我觉得安心。我用银杏叶作为反复出现的意象，象征疗愈与家庭和睦，并把它们排成圆形，寓意圆满与守护。温暖的黄色调和柔和的水彩般质感营造出温柔、梦幻的氛围——这是一种视觉语言，映照着被一代代传下来的爱所带来的慰藉。
