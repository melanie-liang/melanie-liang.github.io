I'm Simei Liang (Melanie), a RISD illustrator and curator working at the intersection of image-making and cultural programming.

As an Illustrator

My work explores identity, memory, and belonging. It has been recognized by the Society of Illustrators LA (SILA), the Society of Illustrators NY, and the 3×3 International Illustration Annual, where I received a Bronze award and the Gary Powell Award for Young Illustrators.

As a Curator & Arts Organizer

Beyond the studio, I organize exhibitions and cross-institutional events. As Vice President of RISD's CSSA, I co-curated the RISD China Exhibition Tour in Hangzhou and participated in Beijing art exhibitions — both as a contributing artist and as part of the curatorial team. I've also led media campaigns for international art festivals and coordinated programming for film festivals and gallery events.

I believe art thrives at the intersection of creation and connection. That's where I want to work.
