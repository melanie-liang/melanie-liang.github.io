Melanie (Simei Liang) is a RISD illustrator and curator.Melanie（Simei Liang）是罗德岛设计学院（RISD）的插画师与策展人。

As an Illustrator作为插画师

Her work explores identity, memory, and belonging. It has been recognized by the Society of Illustrators LA (SILA), the Society of Illustrators NY, and the 3×3 International Illustration Annual, where she received a Bronze award and the Gary Powell Award for Young Illustrators, and has been featured on the social media of Rhode Island School of Design and 3×3 magazine.她的作品探讨身份、记忆与归属。作品曾获洛杉矶插画家协会（SILA）、纽约插画家协会以及 3×3 国际插画年鉴的认可，并在 3×3 年鉴中获得铜奖和 Gary Powell 青年插画师奖；作品也曾在罗德岛设计学院和 3×3 杂志的社交媒体上发布。

As a Curator & Arts Organizer作为策展人与艺术活动组织者

Beyond the studio, she organizes exhibitions and cross-institutional events. As Vice President of RISD's CSSA, she co-curated the RISD China Exhibition Tour in Hangzhou and participated in Beijing art exhibitions — both as a contributing artist and as part of the curatorial team. She has also led media campaigns for international art festivals and coordinated programming for film festivals and gallery events.在创作之外，她也组织展览和跨院校活动。作为 RISD 中国学生学者联合会（CSSA）副主席，她联合策划了 RISD 中国巡展杭州站，并参与了北京的艺术展览——既是参展艺术家，也是策展团队的一员。她还曾为国际艺术节主导媒体宣传，并为电影节和画廊活动统筹活动安排。
