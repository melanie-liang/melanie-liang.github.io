Melanie (Simei Liang) is a RISD illustrator and curator.

As an Illustrator

Her work explores identity, memory, and belonging. It has been recognized by the Society of Illustrators LA (SILA), the Society of Illustrators NY, and the 3×3 International Illustration Annual, where she received a Bronze award and the Gary Powell Award for Young Illustrators, and has been featured on the social media of Rhode Island School of Design and 3×3 magazine.

As a Curator & Arts Organizer

Beyond the studio, she organizes exhibitions and cross-institutional events. As Vice President of RISD's CSSA, she co-curated the RISD China Exhibition Tour in Hangzhou and participated in Beijing art exhibitions — both as a contributing artist and as part of the curatorial team. She has also led media campaigns for international art festivals and coordinated programming for film festivals and gallery events.
