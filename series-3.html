Fruit Skin

2023–2026 · Color Pencil2023–2026 · 彩色铅笔

This series merges fruit skin with human skin — orange peel as stretch marks, kiwi fuzz as body hair, lychee shell as dry skin, and strawberry seeds as blackheads. The work explores how skin is often labeled, categorized, and judged — particularly on women's bodies. By placing fruit and human skin side by side, the series invites viewers to reconsider what they see as natural or unnatural. Visually, I used color pencil with high-saturation, vibrant tones to create strong contrast and visual impact.这个系列把水果的表皮与人的皮肤融合在一起——橙子皮是膨胀纹，猕猴桃的绒毛是体毛，荔枝壳是干燥的皮肤，草莓籽是黑头。作品探讨皮肤如何常常被贴上标签、被分类、被评判——尤其是女性的身体。通过把水果与人的皮肤并置，这个系列邀请观者重新思考：自己眼中什么是自然的，什么又是不自然的。视觉上，我用高饱和、鲜艳的彩色铅笔，营造出强烈的对比和视觉冲击。
