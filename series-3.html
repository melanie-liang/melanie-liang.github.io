Fruit Skin

2023–2026 · Color Pencil

This series merges fruit skin with human skin — orange peel as stretch marks, kiwi fuzz as body hair, lychee shell as dry skin, and strawberry seeds as blackheads. The work explores how skin is often labeled, categorized, and judged — particularly on women's bodies. By placing fruit and human skin side by side, the series invites viewers to reconsider what they see as natural or unnatural. Visually, I used color pencil with high-saturation, vibrant tones to create strong contrast and visual impact.
